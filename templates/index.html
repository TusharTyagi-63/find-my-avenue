Find My Avenue

A hazard-aware route planning prototype that combines live traffic viewing, saved road hazard reports, road-video analysis, and simulated emergency response support.

Safer Routing Dashboard
Compare alternate routes, turn on live traffic, and score each path against the saved road hazards collected from earlier analyses.

Road Hazard Detection
Upload road clips, tag the road location, and build a growing hazard map that can influence later route recommendations.

Emergency Response Module
Create simulated emergency alerts, connect them to hazard reports, and show how police, ambulance, or rescue services would be notified.
